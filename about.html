About SwiftStreamAI

SwiftStreamAI helps small service businesses answer every call and reply to every lead, using AI that works around the clock.

Why I started it

I am Sage Stone, a Computer Information Systems student at Arizona State University and the founder of SwiftStreamAI. I started the company after seeing how many calls and leads small businesses miss simply because everyone is busy doing the actual work.

A dentist cannot answer the phone during a procedure, and a plumber cannot reply to a text from under a sink. I wanted to build tools that handle those moments so the business never has to choose between the customer in front of them and the one calling.

What we focus on

We work with owner operated businesses such as dental practices, veterinary clinics, med spas, and home service companies. These businesses depend on the phone, and a missed call is often a lost customer.

  • Based in: Phoenix, Arizona
  • Products: AI Receptionist, Speed to Lead, and Website packages
  • Built for: Dental, veterinary, med spa, and home service businesses
  • Approach: Simple setup, flat pricing, and no long term contract
